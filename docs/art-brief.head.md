# Tennis Stars: art brief

Everything in the game that wants art, with file names, sizes, prompts and how it animates.
Drop finished files into `public/art/<folder>/<file>`. **Wired today:** card art (`art/cards/`) and pack art
(`art/packs/`) are picked up automatically, with the CSS/vector design as fallback. Every other slot below
(faces, trinkets, venues, key art…) has its fallback in place and gets wired as its art arrives, so art can be
delivered in any order.

This file is generated by `npx tsx scripts/art-brief.ts` from `docs/art-brief.head.md` plus the game data.

## 1. Style guide (use for every prompt)

- **Look:** premium sports-videogame UI, original style (do not reference or imitate an existing game).
  Clean illustrated shapes with a soft 3D feel, studio rim light from upper left, slight bloom on highlights.
- **Palette:** deep navy `#06122e` / `#0b1f4a`, royal blue `#1d58b8`, sky `#3b8cff`; accents orange
  `#ff7a3d` → peach `#ffb36b`, teal `#3ee0cf`, yellow `#ffd84a`.
- **Rarity colours** (cards, packs, trinkets, frames): Common `#8fa0b5`, Uncommon `#2fd07a`, Rare `#3b8cff`,
  Epic `#a865ff`, Legendary `#ffc22e`. Packs wear the colour of the best rarity they usually give:
  Club = green, Tour = blue, Pro = purple, Champion = gold.
- **Never:** text baked into images (the game adds all text), real brands, logos, real players' likenesses,
  watermarks.
- **Formats:** WebP (or PNG with alpha where noted), sRGB. Sizes below are the export size; 2× is welcome.
- **Base style suffix** appended to the prompts below:

  > {{STYLE}}

## 2. Faces (realistic, assembled from pieces)

Faces are semi-realistic painted portraits assembled in the game from a few large pieces. Realistic eyes, noses and
mouths taken from different heads never match in lighting and skin, so **each head is one complete face** and the
variety comes from combining heads with hair, facial hair, accessories and kit colours. 30 heads × 20 hair styles ×
10 hair colours × 5 facial-hair options × 5 accessories gives tens of thousands of distinct players.

### 2.1 Framing (identical for every piece)

The approved anchor is `docs/art-templates/face-anchor.png`, and `face-anchor-guide.png` marks its lines. Every
piece matches it exactly:

- Canvas **1024 × 1024 px**, front-facing head and shoulders, camera at eye height, head centred on x = 512.
- Top of skull y ≈ 150 · brows ≈ 370 · **eye line 405** (pupils at x ≈ 425 and 600) · nose base ≈ 512 · mouth ≈ 578 ·
  **chin ≈ 700**.
- Lighting as in the anchor: warm key light from the upper left, cool teal rim light on the right, plain grey background.
- Expression: calm and focused, mouth closed, looking into the camera.

### 2.2 Style anchor (done)

`face-anchor.png`. Use it as the reference image (image prompt or style reference) for every piece below, with
the same model, seed family and settings. Its prompt, for regenerating or for new heads:

> semi-realistic digital painting of a young professional tennis player, front-facing head-and-shoulders portrait,
> looking straight at the camera, calm focused expression, mouth closed, warm studio key light from upper left, subtle
> teal rim light on the right, painterly but realistic skin, clean edges, plain mid-grey background, modern
> sports videogame player card art, high detail, centred, symmetrical framing

**Negative prompt:** cartoon, chibi, anime, big eyes, caricature, 3D render, plastic skin, photo, text, logo, watermark,
jewellery, tilted head, smiling with teeth, hands, harsh shadows, real person likeness

### 2.2b Feature pieces: eyes, nose and mouth (`faces/eyes/<tone>-<n>.png`, `faces/nose/…`, `faces/mouth/…`)

Swapping features works when every feature is **inpainted on a head of the same skin tone, at the anchor's exact
position**, so lighting and skin match. Mask only the box, keep the rest of the head identical, then save the box
region as a transparent PNG with a soft 24 px feathered edge. Boxes on the 1024 canvas:

| Piece | Box (x1, y1 → x2, y2) | Includes | Prompt for [feature] |
|---|---|---|---|
| eyes | 340, 335 → 684, 455 | both eyes and eyebrows | *[deep-set / almond / hooded / round / narrow] [brown / hazel / green / blue / grey] eyes, [thick straight / arched / thin / bushy] eyebrows* |
| nose | 448, 415 → 576, 535 | bridge, tip, nostrils | *[straight narrow / broad / small upturned / aquiline / button] nose* |
| mouth | 428, 545 → 596, 628 | lips and the skin around them | *[full / thin / wide / small, bow-shaped] lips, mouth closed, neutral* |

Make 4–6 of each per skin-tone group (start with 3 per group). The game combines a head with any eyes, nose and
mouth of the same tone, so 3 heads × 5 eyes × 5 noses × 5 mouths = 375 faces per tone before hair.

### 2.3 Heads (`faces/head/<tour>-<tone>-<n>.png`, full colour)

A head is the complete face, ears and neck with **no hair** (a smooth bald scalp with a hint of shaved stubble) and
eyebrows included. Paint a plain mid-grey crew-neck shirt; the game recolours it to the kit. Make 3 per skin tone
and tour (30 in total; one per tone and tour, 10 in total, is enough to start). Tone groups match the game's 10 skin tones in pairs:
`1` very fair · `2` fair · `3` olive / light brown · `4` brown · `5` deep brown.

> [style anchor prompt], **[man | woman]** aged about **[18 | 23 | 28]**, **[tone description]** skin,
> **[face shape: oval | square jaw | long narrow | round | high cheekbones and pointed chin]**, **[eye shape and
> colour]**, **[nose: straight | broad | small upturned | aquiline]**, completely bald smooth head with no hair,
> natural eyebrows, plain grey crew-neck t-shirt, same framing, lighting and camera as the reference

Vary ethnicity naturally within each tone (e.g. tone 3 covers Mediterranean, Latin American, South Asian and East
Asian faces). Keep the eye line and chin on the template lines; check by overlaying the template at 50% opacity.

**Optional extras per head:** `<head>-happy.png` (relaxed smile, for wins) and `<head>-tired.png` (sweaty, frowning,
for losses), made by inpainting only the mouth and brows on the same head.

### 2.4 Hair (`faces/hairFront/<style>.png`, `faces/hairBack/<style>.png`, neutral grey)

Generate hair **on the approved bald anchor head** by inpainting the hair area only, then cut it out to a transparent
PNG (background removal plus a hand-cleaned edge, keep fine flyaway strands). Paint it in **neutral grey**: base tone
`#808080`, highlights up to `#e0e0e0`, shadows down to `#202020`. The game multiplies it by 10 hair colours, so one
file covers everyone. Hair that falls behind the shoulders or neck (ponytails, long hair) goes in a separate
`hairBack` file drawn behind the head. Cover the scalp generously past the hairline so it fits all 30 heads.

> [style anchor prompt], **[hairstyle]**, hair rendered in neutral grey tones only, realistic strands and volume,
> same head, framing and lighting as the reference, transparent background

{{FACE_LAYERS}}

### 2.5 Facial hair, accessories and kit

- **Facial hair** (`faces/facialHair/<n>.png`, neutral grey like hair, ATP only): `1` light stubble (semi-transparent),
  `2` short full beard, `3` moustache, `4` goatee. Inpaint on the anchor head, then cut out.
- **Accessories** (`faces/accessory/<name>.png`, neutral light grey, re-coloured to the kit): `headband` (terry
  sweatband across the forehead), `cap` (sport cap, brim forward, sits above the eyebrows), `visor`, `bandana` (tied
  at the back). For the cap, hairFront is hidden; supply `faces/hairFront/<style>-cap.png` if the hair should show
  under it.
- **Kit** (`faces/shirt/polo.png`, neutral grey): a tennis polo with collar and placket to replace the crew neck.

### 2.6 How the game assembles them

Back to front: `background · hairBack · head (incl. shirt) · eyes · nose · mouth · facialHair · hairFront · accessory`. The game picks a head
from the player's tour and skin tone, then hair, facial hair and accessory from their genes, tints the grey pieces and
draws them on a canvas (a small compositor in `src/career/faces.ts`, about 50 lines, to be added when the first
pieces arrive). Pieces are listed in `public/art/faces/manifest.json`, e.g.
`{ "head": ["atp-3-1", "wta-2-1"], "hairFront": ["crop", "ponytail"], "hairBack": ["ponytail"] }`. Until a piece
exists, the drawn vector face is used, so art can arrive in any order.

**Animation:** a subtle idle (0.5° head bob, slow breathing scale) on the match intro and month review; the `-happy`
and `-tired` heads are swapped in on Victory and Defeat.

## 3. Cards

Card frames, rarity glow, holographic sweep and stat chips are drawn in code. Art fills the art window
(**768 × 768 px**, square, subject centred with 10% margin; the card crops it softly at the edges).
**Animation (in code):** cards fly in with a small rotation, lift and tilt on hover or tap, and flip in on
pack reveal; Epic and Legendary have a looping holographic sweep and Legendary pulses gold and shakes when revealed.
Optional extra: a 6-frame sprite strip `cards/<id>-anim.webp` (6 × 768 px wide) for a gentle loop
(flames flicker, sparkles drift) on Epic and Legendary cards.

## 16. Animation summary (all in code unless art frames are supplied)

- **Everywhere:** page slide transitions, sliding tab indicator, tap ripple, count-up numbers, bars that fill.
- **Week:** event cards stagger in; hero tilts toward the pointer; Play button shine.
- **Match:** head-to-head bars fill; live court rally per game; "Break!" and "Hold" calls; score bug digits flip;
  set-end overlay; Victory with confetti.
- **Cards and packs:** float, shine, shake, burst, flip; holographic sweep on Epic and Legendary.
- **Month in review:** story-style slides (month title slam, results, ranking ticker, skill bars growing,
  bank count-up, unlock pops), auto-advancing with a progress bar.
- **Season review and legacy:** stat cards stagger in, grade slams in, confetti for good seasons.
