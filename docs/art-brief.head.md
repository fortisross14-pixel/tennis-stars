# Tennis Stars: art brief

Everything in the game that wants art, with file names, sizes, prompts and how it animates.
Drop finished files into `public/art/<folder>/<file>`. **Wired today:** card art (`art/cards/`) and pack art
(`art/packs/`) are picked up automatically, with the CSS/vector design as fallback. Every other slot below
(faces, trinkets, venues, key art…) has its fallback in place and gets wired as its art arrives, so art can be
delivered in any order.

This file is generated by `npx tsx scripts/art-brief.ts` from `docs/art-brief.head.md` plus the game data.

## 1. Style guide (use for every prompt)

- **Look:** premium sports-videogame UI, original style (do not reference or imitate an existing game).
  Clean illustrated shapes with a soft 3D feel, studio rim light from upper left, slight bloom on highlights.
- **Palette:** deep navy `#06122e` / `#0b1f4a`, royal blue `#1d58b8`, sky `#3b8cff`; accents orange
  `#ff7a3d` → peach `#ffb36b`, teal `#3ee0cf`, yellow `#ffd84a`.
- **Rarity colours** (cards, packs, trinkets, frames): Common `#8fa0b5`, Uncommon `#2fd07a`, Rare `#3b8cff`,
  Epic `#a865ff`, Legendary `#ffc22e`. Packs wear the colour of the best rarity they usually give:
  Club = green, Tour = blue, Pro = purple, Champion = gold.
- **Never:** text baked into images (the game adds all text), real brands, logos, real players' likenesses,
  watermarks.
- **Formats:** WebP (or PNG with alpha where noted), sRGB. Sizes below are the export size; 2× is welcome.
- **Base style suffix** appended to the prompts below:

  > {{STYLE}}

## 2. Faces (procedural, part-based)

Faces are assembled in code from layers in a 200 × 200 unit box (export art at **1024 × 1024 px**, so 1 unit
= 5.12 px). Order, back to front:

`background · hairBack · shirt · neck · ears · head · eyes · brows · nose · mouth · facialHair · hairFront · accessory`

- Templates in `docs/art-templates/` (made by `node scripts/face-templates.cjs`) are **placement guides only, not
  a style reference**: `face-template-atp.png` / `face-template-wta.png` are plain grey mannequin silhouettes
  (head, ears, neck, shoulders) and `face-layer-guide.png` marks the skull top, hairline, brows, eye line, nose
  tip, mouth and chin. Don't feed them to a generator as a style image.
- **Style anchor first:** generate one complete face in the look you want, e.g. *front-facing head-and-shoulders
  portrait of a young professional tennis player, stylized semi-realistic sports videogame art, soft studio rim
  light, neutral grey background, centred, looking at camera*. Scale it so it matches the mannequin's outline,
  then inpaint hair and facial hair away to get your base head. Build every part on that base.
- **Tinted layers** (skin and hair) must be painted in **neutral grey**, with `#808080` as the base tone and
  shading lighter or darker around it. The game multiplies them by 10 skin tones and 10 hair colours, so one
  sheet covers everyone. Eyes, mouth, shirt and accessories are painted in full colour (shirt and accessory are
  re-coloured to the country kit in code, so paint them neutral grey too).
- Each file is a full 1024 × 1024 transparent PNG with the part in its final position (no cropping), named
  `public/art/faces/<layer>/<variant>.png`, and listed in `public/art/faces/manifest.json`, e.g.
  `{ "head": ["0","1","2","3","4"], "hairFront": ["crop","ponytail"] }`.
- **How to generate parts that line up:** inpaint on your base head (from the style anchor), checking it against
  the guide. For hair: mask only the hair area and prompt the hairstyle; then cut the hair out to its own
  transparent PNG. For eyes, nose and mouth, mask that region only. Keep the same seed and model for a whole
  layer so the set looks consistent.
- **Generic face-part prompt:**
  > front-facing stylized athlete bust, *[part description]*, neutral grey material (#808080) with soft
  > shading, same camera, framing and lighting as the base head, clean edges,
  > transparent background, {{STYLE}}

{{FACE_LAYERS}}

Engineering note: the vector faces render as SVG images today. Once PNG parts exist, the renderer switches to a
small canvas compositor (an image inside an SVG used as `<img>` cannot load external files); that change is
about 50 lines in `src/career/faces.ts`.

**Animation:** faces get a subtle idle (0.5° head bob, blink every 4–7 s by swapping the eyes layer for
an `eyes/<n>-closed.png` frame) on the match intro and month review; a happy and a frustrated mouth variant
(`mouth/happy.png`, `mouth/angry.png`) are used on Victory and Defeat.

## 3. Cards

Card frames, rarity glow, holographic sweep and stat chips are drawn in code. Art fills the art window
(**768 × 768 px**, square, subject centred with 10% margin; the card crops it softly at the edges).
**Animation (in code):** cards fly in with a small rotation, lift and tilt on hover or tap, and flip in on
pack reveal; Epic and Legendary have a looping holographic sweep and Legendary pulses gold and shakes when revealed.
Optional extra: a 6-frame sprite strip `cards/<id>-anim.webp` (6 × 768 px wide) for a gentle loop
(flames flicker, sparkles drift) on Epic and Legendary cards.

## 14. Tournament marks

~150 tournaments: generated in code as crests (shape by level, colour by surface, initials). Hand-made art only
for the landmarks, **512 × 512 px** transparent emblems, no text: the four Slams, Tour Finals, Olympics,
Davis/BJK Cup, and one emblem per level (ITF, Challenger/125, 250, 500, 1000, Junior Slam, Junior 500/200).
Prompt: *a tennis tournament emblem, [motif], shield or roundel, metallic, {{STYLE}}, transparent background*.

## 16. Animation summary (all in code unless art frames are supplied)

- **Everywhere:** page slide transitions, sliding tab indicator, tap ripple, count-up numbers, bars that fill.
- **Week:** event cards stagger in; hero tilts toward the pointer; Play button shine.
- **Match:** head-to-head bars fill; live court rally per game; "Break!" and "Hold" calls; score bug digits flip;
  set-end overlay; Victory with confetti.
- **Cards and packs:** float, shine, shake, burst, flip; holographic sweep on Epic and Legendary.
- **Month in review:** story-style slides (month title slam, results, ranking ticker, skill bars growing,
  bank count-up, unlock pops), auto-advancing with a progress bar.
- **Season review and legacy:** stat cards stagger in, grade slams in, confetti for good seasons.
